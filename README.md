## Research Summary
This assignment focuses on identifying Federer-profile manufacturing companies operating in Pune within the Industrial Sensors & Instrumentation segment.

Companies were evaluated using the C1-C6 Federer criteria based on:
- Manufacturing capability
- India presence
- Product differentiation
- Technical leadership
- Growth sector relevance
- Active growth indicators

Research sources included:
- Company websites
- LinkedIn
- Google Search
- Industry directories
- Automation company listings

AI tools were used for structuring and organizing information while company relevance was manually reviewed and verified.
