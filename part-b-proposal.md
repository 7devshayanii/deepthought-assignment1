/# Part B Proposal

## Question 1: Sourcing Methods

### 1. LinkedIn
I would use LinkedIn to find manufacturing companies in automation and instrumentation sectors. Hiring activity and company pages help identify growing companies.

### 2. IndiaMART and TradeIndia
These websites help find Indian manufacturers based on product categories and industrial segments.

### 3. Company Websites
I would check company websites for:
- products
- manufacturing plants
- certifications
- expansion news
- automation solutions

### 4. Industrial Exhibitions
I would use exhibitor lists from Automation Expo, IMTEX and industrial trade events because many growing manufacturing companies participate there.

### 5. Google Search and Industry Directories
I would use Google search and industrial directories to find specialty manufacturers in Pune and other industrial cities.
